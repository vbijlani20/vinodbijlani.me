How much does an AI answer really cost? The token bill is only one part of the economics.

**Tokenomics** measures the cost and speed of generating tokens. Track cost per million tokens, tokens per second, time to first token and GPU utilisation, along with what drives token demand (prompt length, output and agent loops) and how efficiently you serve models (size, precision, caching, batching and routing).

**Datanomics** measures the cost of making data useful for AI. Track the cost and time to make data AI-ready, trusted data coverage across quality, lineage and access, retrieval quality, data freshness and security costs, and the cost of evaluation, errors and rework.

Tokenomics tells us what it costs to run AI. Datanomics tells us what it costs to make AI useful and trustworthy. Together, they show more of the **total cost of enterprise AI**.

The lowest cost per million tokens doesn't always mean the lowest cost per trusted decision. Data readiness, governance, retrieval, lineage and sovereignty belong in AI's unit economics too.

Which of these measures are you tracking today, and what would you add?
