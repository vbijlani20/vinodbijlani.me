**Australia's data centre capacity is projected to more than double by 2030. The bigger shift is what we build on top of it.** After looking at Asia's data centre expansion and India's AI infrastructure ambitions, I took a closer look at Australia and where it can carve out its own advantage.

**A different path to AI scale.** Every APAC market brings a different mix of energy, land, capital and demand. For Australia, a compelling opportunity is connecting renewable energy and regional land with sovereign AI demand. That creates a path beyond metropolitan expansion, to regional AI factories designed around energy availability from the outset.

**The scale of the shift.** Operational capacity was around 1.4 GW in 2025 and is projected to reach around 3.2 GW by 2030. Behind those numbers is a transformation spanning hyperscale campuses, sovereign compute and regional AI factories.

**Major players shaping the landscape.** CDC provides secure infrastructure for sovereign enterprise and government compute. AirTrunk builds hyperscale campuses for cloud and high-density AI. Firmus is developing renewable-powered AI factories in Tasmania and South Australia. NEXTDC, Equinix and Digital Realty supply the colocation and connectivity that support the broader ecosystem.

**The geography is evolving.** Sydney and Melbourne remain central, but regional developments bring another equation into focus: **power + land + cooling + connectivity.** Renewable energy is an advantage. Converting it into reliable compute requires firm power, transmission, cooling and community support.

**From capacity to capability.** That means power and cooling engineered for dense GPU clusters, AI platforms that turn infrastructure into usable services, security and governance from the start, and operating models that connect utilisation to cost per token.

Announced megawatts are only the beginning. The real test is how much capacity becomes productive compute, and how much of that compute creates business value. Australia's opportunity lies in bringing the complete AI supply chain together: **energy, infrastructure, platforms, data and talent.** That is how a data centre buildout becomes lasting sovereign AI capability.
