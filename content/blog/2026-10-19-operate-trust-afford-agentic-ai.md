One question followed me from Taipei to Hong Kong:

**How do we make agentic AI something an enterprise can operate, trust and afford?**

Across two stops of HPE Discover More, around 25 customer meetings, three panel discussions, two CxO luncheons and one keynote, four themes kept coming up:

1. **Token consumption**, as agents take on more work
2. **LLM sprawl**, across teams and applications
3. **Security**, as agents access data and take action
4. **Governance**, over permissions, decisions and accountability

For me, this is the shift from the AI factory to the **governed token factory**: from building AI capacity to running it as a governed, metered service.

We need visibility into token consumption. Control over models and agent actions. And a clear view of what each useful outcome costs. That brings infrastructure, economics and governance into the same conversation.

Thank you to our HPE teams in Taiwan and Hong Kong, and to our customers, partners and fellow panellists for the thoughtful discussions.

Which is the biggest challenge in your organisation: consumption, model sprawl, security or governance?
