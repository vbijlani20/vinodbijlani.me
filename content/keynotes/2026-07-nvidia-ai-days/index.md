A year ago the question was "Which LLM should we use?". Now it is "How do we securely operationalise AI and scale it across the enterprise?"

My sessions covered building AI that is secure, governed and compliant by design, moving from pilots to enterprise-scale adoption, and measuring AI economics beyond cost per million tokens, including the cost per unit of intelligence delivered. I shared how HPE Services for AI Workshops and Services Accelerators for HPE Private Cloud AI help organisations get there.
