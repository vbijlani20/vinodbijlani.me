Building an AI factory is not just about deploying GPUs or choosing the right LLM. It is about an enterprise AI platform that is open, secure, governed and ready for AI agents.

We discussed what a sovereign AI factory really looks like, the technology stack needed to operate AI at enterprise scale, how open, secure and hybrid architectures accelerate adoption, and why governance and operational control matter as much as model performance.
