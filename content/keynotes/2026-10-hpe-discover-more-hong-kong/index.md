Two panels at HPE Discover More Hong Kong.

**Leading Through the AI Era: Navigating Transformation in an Age of Intelligence.** I moderated a conversation with Flora Ng, CIO and Chief Librarian at HKU, and Patrick Khong, Chief Data Officer at the Hong Kong Jockey Club. Our three takeaways:

1. **Prepare people for change.** Build the skills and confidence to use AI responsibly.
2. **Build on trusted foundations.** Good data and clear governance let organisations use AI with confidence.
3. **Keep humans in the lead, not just in the loop.** Human judgement and accountability must guide how AI is applied.

**From AI Factory to Token Economy: Building the Governed, Connected AI Enterprise**, with Prem Naraindas, CEO of Katonic AI, and Aaron Tong, HPE Networking. The price of a token is only part of the equation; what matters is the cost of completing a task safely and well. We discussed thinking in systems, why networking shapes performance, building trust into agent workflows, measuring the whole task and starting with a valuable workflow.
