Keynote, customer meetings and CxO conversations across HPE Discover More Taipei and Hong Kong: around 25 customer meetings, 3 panel discussions, 2 CxO luncheons and 1 keynote.

One question followed me from Taipei to Hong Kong: how do we make agentic AI something an enterprise can operate, trust and afford? Four themes kept coming up: token consumption, LLM sprawl, security and governance. For me this is the shift from the AI factory to the **governed token factory**: from building AI capacity to running it as a governed, metered service.
