Why is the HPE AI Factory the most secure, compliant and agentic AI factory? Presented with Soon Heng at HPE's APAC solution sales bootcamp in Bangkok.

Enterprise AI is at a clear inflection point. 2024 was the move from GPT wrappers to private AI, and 2025 pulled models behind the firewall. 2026 is about execution, accountability and action: deploying AI with governance, financial control and operational ownership, not experimenting or planning.

The HPE AI Factory is a production blueprint for enterprise AI, built on four pillars:

1. **Security by design.** Security is designed in, not layered on: prompt injection defence, command injection controls, SOC integration and cyber resilience across the stack.
2. **Compliance built in.** AI without traceability is a risk: a Data Bill of Materials (DBOM), traceability and regional regulatory considerations.
3. **Financial and operational control.** If you cannot measure it, you cannot scale it: token governance, user-level accountability, workload visibility and cost transparency.
4. **Agentic workflows.** Where the real transformation begins: workflows that reason, orchestrate and take action within governed boundaries, not just generate text.

We are moving past the era of renting intelligence from public endpoints. The future of enterprise AI is private AI that brings the model to the data, not the data to the model, and brings execution discipline to AI systems that can act.
