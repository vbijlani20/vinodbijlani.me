Two sessions at the w.media Sydney Cloud & Datacenter Convention.

**Keynote: Edge AI & Real-Time Decision Making: Transforming the Intelligent Enterprise.** Physical AI starts with edge AI: models that perceive and decide locally, agents that reason and act without waiting on the cloud, and eventually robots and autonomous systems. The keynote set out six design requirements for a distributed AI factory, from centralised AI governance to low-latency local inference, and a reference architecture that runs micro AI factories in stores and on factory floors, governed from a central AI factory. Use cases spanned real-time video analytics, visual quality inspection, predictive maintenance and a virtual store manager: an agentic assistant at the edge of every store that answers most questions locally, even offline.

**Panel: Neo Clouds vs Hyperscalers: The New Battle for AI Infrastructure**, moderated by Rana Qadir (Metrics Infrastructure & Services) with Joseph Vijay (Leading Edge Data Centres), Anthony Woodward (OrionVM) and John Henderson (Centuria DC). The question that shapes most AI workload-placement decisions is not cloud versus on-premises. It is which mix of hyperscaler, neocloud, self-hosted, colocation and edge fits each workload's cost, latency, sovereignty and scale.
