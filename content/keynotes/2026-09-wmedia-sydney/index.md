A keynote on AI-specific security and data sovereignty by design, followed by the panel "Neo Clouds vs Hyperscalers: The New Battle for AI Infrastructure", moderated by Rana Qadir (Metrics Infrastructure & Services) with Joseph Vijay (Leading Edge Data Centres), Anthony Woodward (OrionVM) and John Henderson (Centuria DC).

The question that shapes most AI workload-placement decisions is not cloud versus on-premises. It is which mix of hyperscaler, neocloud, self-hosted, colocation and edge fits each workload's cost, latency, sovereignty and scale.
