Keynote at SUSE Summit Sydney, Hilton Sydney, on 11 August 2026.

The session linked the AI factory's cloud-to-edge architecture to use cases already driving adoption, from real-time video analytics to a virtual store manager agent. It walked through the HPE SUSE AI Factory central reference architecture, where the core handles training, governance and fleet control for every site, and the key design requirements for a distributed AI factory: centralised AI governance, low-latency inference where data is created, small language models on compact edge hardware, security, data sovereignty by design, and resilient, autonomous reasoning when the link goes down.

It also covered the shift towards physical AI, and the practical steps that turn edge AI pilots into enterprise deployments: data readiness, infrastructure, governance and scaling.
